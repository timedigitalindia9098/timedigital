GST Calculator Online: Simplify Your Business Taxes

In the Indian taxation system, GST (Goods and Services Tax) is a crucial component for every business transaction. Whether you are a small business owner in Indore, a freelancer, or a retail shopkeeper, calculating the exact GST amount is vital for professional and legal invoicing.

Understanding Standard GST Slabs in India:

  • 5% GST: Generally applied to essential household items, basic clothing, and basic necessities.
  • 12% & 18% GST: The most common standard rates applied to a vast majority of FMCG goods, IT services, and general products.
  • 28% GST: Specifically reserved for luxury items, automobiles, and high-end electronics.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is GST calculated?

GST is calculated by multiplying the taxable amount by the applicable GST rate (5%, 12%, 18%, or 28%) and dividing by 100. Our online tool automates this mathematical process instantly.
