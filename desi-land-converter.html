How many Square Feet are in local units?

Unlike global units, regional units vary. Here is the standard conversion chart used by property dealers and state governments:

Local Unit Standard Equivalent (Approx.) Used Mostly In
1 Gaj (Square Yard) 9 Square Feet Delhi, Haryana, Punjab

Note on Bigha: The size of a Bigha varies heavily by state. While this tool uses the standard UP/Rajasthan measurement (27,000 sq ft) for general calculations, always verify localized state revenue records for exact legal property registrations.
