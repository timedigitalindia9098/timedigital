Bijoy to Unicode Converter

Instantly convert your legacy Bangla Bijoy text into standard Unicode format. 100% free, runs entirely in your browser with zero daily limits.

How to use this tool:

Simply paste your Bijoy Bangla text into the left box (or top box on mobile), and click "Convert to Unicode". The accurately converted Unicode text will appear instantly in the right box.

Why Use Our Free Bijoy to Unicode Tool?

If you are working with old documents, government files, or legacy software in Bangladesh and West Bengal, you often encounter text typed in the Bijoy keyboard layout. Converting this to a web-friendly format can be frustrating when dealing with annoying popups and forced sign-ups.
