Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How to compress a photo to 50kb for government exams?

Simply upload your passport-size photo, select the "SSC / Govt Exam Photo (Max 50KB)" preset from the dropdown, and our algorithm will automatically resize the width, height, and quality to ensure the final output is below 50 kilobytes.

What is the required size for a signature in Sarkari forms?

For most government exams, including SSC, RRB, and State PSCs, the signature file must be between 10KB and 20KB in JPG or JPEG format. Our "Govt Exam Signature (Max 20KB)" preset handles this perfectly in one click.
