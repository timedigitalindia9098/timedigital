How Does Our EMI Calculator Work?

Managing your finances starts with understanding your liabilities. Whether you are planning to buy a dream home, a new car, or taking a personal loan, calculating your Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) beforehand is crucial. Our free EMI calculator helps you plan your budget efficiently.

The mathematical formula used by banks to calculate EMI is:
EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n / ((1 + r)n - 1)

  • P = Principal loan amount
  • r = Rate of interest calculated per month
  • n = Loan tenure in months

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is an EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It is a fixed payment amount made by a borrower to a lender at a specified date each calendar month. EMIs are used to pay off both interest and principal each month so that over a specified number of years, the loan is paid off in full.

How is EMI calculated?

The mathematical formula to calculate EMI is: EMI = [P x R x (1+R)^N]/[(1+R)^N-1], where P stands for the loan amount or principal, R is the interest rate per month, and N is the number of monthly installments.

Does the EMI amount change during the loan tenure?

If you opt for a fixed interest rate, your EMI remains the same throughout the loan tenure. However, if you choose a floating interest rate (usually common in Home Loans), your EMI or loan tenure may fluctuate depending on the RBI's repo rate and market interest rates.
